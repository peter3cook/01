Yellowhammer Creative / Brand exploration, round 01
October 2026 — for approval

Three directions. One ambition.

The brief: make a studio whose website packages start at £199 look like one that charges £5,000. Premium, established and design-led. Never generic, never cheap.

Each row on this canvas is one direction: the identity system on the left, the homepage hero on the right. Our recommendation is at the end.

01 — Field research: what outstanding studios do
Pentagram

The work is the interface. Minimal chrome, very large project imagery, almost no sales language.

We take → confidence through restraint.

Collins

Concept before cosmetics. Every identity rides on one sharp, ownable idea that everything else serves.

We take → a single brand idea we can own.

Koto

Warm, colourful and rigorously systematic. Brands that feel human but hold together at scale.

We take → personality without losing polish.

Porto Rocha

Expressive, oversized typography as the hero. Type does the job that stock photography does elsewhere.

We take → type that carries a whole page.

Ragged Edge

Plain-spoken, witty, challenger-brand copy. You can read the whole strategy in one line.

We take → tone of voice as a design asset.

Locomotive

Engineering-grade web craft. Smooth motion and obsessive interaction detail signal the price tag.

We take → polish you feel, not just see.

02 — What makes affordable look cheap
✕  Stock photos of handshakes and laptops
✕  Centred hero, then three icon cards, repeated forever
✕  Purple-to-blue gradients, glass panels, sparkles
✕  Every section the same width and rhythm
✕  Discount-shouting copy: “cheap websites!!”
03 — What signals a £5,000 studio
→  One strong idea, repeated with discipline
→  Typography doing the heavy lifting
→  Generous space and an asymmetric, editorial grid
→  Real work shown large, with a point of view
→  The price stated calmly, as a confident fact
