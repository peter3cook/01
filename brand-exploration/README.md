# Yellowhammer Creative: brand exploration, round 01

Visual canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/3SxxBnVY8duJG9Vbt53tz1 (the source files are in `project/`).

**Brief:** make a studio whose website packages start at £199 look like one that charges £5,000.

## Direction A: All Bread, No Cheese (recommended)
The yellowhammer's song is traditionally written as "a little bit of bread and no cheese". We turn it into a promise: all substance, nothing cheesy. The look is editorial and field-guide inspired: British, crafted and quietly confident.
- **Logo:** a two-tone bird mark (yolk-yellow head, ink body), a serif wordmark, a circular specimen seal and a "Yh" monogram.
- **Colour:** Bone `#EFEAE0`, Ink `#17140F`, Yolk `#F2BF1D`, Chestnut `#8A3E1F`, Hedgerow `#3E4A32`, Fog `#D9D1C1`.
- **Type:** Instrument Serif (display), Instrument Sans (text and UI), DM Mono (annotations).
- **Devices:** broadsheet double rules, "Fig." captions, Roman-numeral plates, "Field Notes" content series.

## Direction B: The Hammer
The name split in two: Yellow is the signal, Hammer is the making. Loud, kinetic and poster-led, with the price stated boldly.
- **Logo:** a stacked condensed wordmark with a square full stop, and a "strike" mark (a hammer that doubles as a T-square).
- **Colour:** Signal `#FFD60A`, Jet `#0A0A0A`, Paper `#FFFFFF`, Concrete `#E4E1D9`, Flare `#FF5A1F`.
- **Type:** Archivo variable (62% width, 900 weight for display), JetBrains Mono.
- **Devices:** a "From £199" price sticker, a ticker band and numbered indexes.

## Direction C: First Light
Across a winter field, all you see of a yellowhammer is one bright point of colour. The look is cinematic, minimal and product-grade, built on a single yellow dot.
- **Logo:** a yellow disc with one dark eye, and a lowercase wordmark.
- **Colour:** Night Field `#0F120D`, Moss `#1B2017`, Linen `#EEE9DD`, Lichen `#8F9A7E`, Pollen `#F6C81E`.
- **Type:** Bricolage Grotesque (display), Geist, Geist Mono.
- **Devices:** the dot used as bullet, cursor and status light, and a rising-sun crop.

## Recommendation
Go with A, borrowing B's bold, price-forward headlines for social and C's calm interface details. A is the only route that makes the low price read as good judgement rather than a compromise.

## Round 2: Direction D, Interface (after feedback: too dark, no yellow needed, more UX)
A light, UX-led identity that uses the language of the design tool itself: frames with selection handles, collaborator cursors, redline measurements and sticky-note comments.
- **Logo:** the lowercase "yellowhammer" wordmark inside a selection frame, a cursor mark labelled "yh", and a framed "yh" app icon.
- **Colour:** Paper `#FAFAF8`, White `#FFFFFF`, Graphite `#18181B`, Slate `#6B6B73`, Selection blue `#2E5BFF`, Redline `#D63D23`. No yellow.
- **Type:** Geist (display and text), Geist Mono (annotations).
- **Hero:** "Websites designed around your customers.", with a mobile mockup on a dotted design canvas and a four-step process strip.
